--- layout: single permalink: / author_profile: true title: "👋About Me" --- Hi! I’m Dr. Zhibin Gao, a specially appointed Researcher and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Materials Science and Engineering, Xi’an Jiaotong University (XJTU), China. I am also affiliated with the State Key Laboratory for Mechanical Behavior of Materials. Prior to joining XJTU, I served as a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (2019–2021) and a Research Scholar at Michigan State University (2017–2018). I earned my Ph.D. in Physics from Tongji University in 2018. I have been honored with several awards, including the Shaanxi Provincial Young 1000 Talent, the Young Talent Support Program by Xi’an Association for Science and Technology, and the XJTU Young Top-notch Talent Program (Category B). I also serve as a Youth Editorial Board Member for the Journal of Materials Informatics.
